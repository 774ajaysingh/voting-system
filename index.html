The Problem (Slide 2)

  • Paper-based elections require more time and manual work.
  • Long queues reduce student convenience and lower participation.
  • Candidate information is scattered or unavailable at voting time.
  • Manual counting introduces delays, disputes, and human errors.
  • Absence of tamper-evident receipts for voters.

Our Solution (Slide 3)

  • Students verify identity, compare candidates, cast one vote, and receive confirmation.
  • Smart Candidate Comparison Matrix establishes fair, identical criteria.
  • Zero-knowledge separation of student identity and ballot choices.
  • Administrators schedule elections, monitor live turnout, and publish results.
  • Real-time department-wise analytics and anomaly detection.
